Our shared principles

How we move
together.

Freedom works best with awareness. You are welcome to move your own way while helping everyone else feel comfortable doing the same.

The session starts with a simple facilitated arrival, rises through a DJ-led musical journey, then lands with a shared closing. Newcomers are welcome. Please arrive by 10:45 am so we can begin together at 11:00 am.

Bring

A water bottle, comfortable layers, a small towel if you expect to sweat, and socks or clean indoor shoes.

Clear heads

This is a substance-free and scent-free space. Please skip perfume, essential oils and other scented products.

Phones away

The dance floor is phone-free. If you need to use your device, please step into the foyer or outside.

Movement & consent

Enjoy your own dance or move with someone else when the invitation is mutual. Look for clear eye contact and body language, and give people room to change their mind at any time.

Quiet floor

We keep conversation off the dance floor so everyone can stay with the music. Step outside the room when you need to chat.

All ages

Kids are warmly welcome and remain in the care of their grown-up. Please keep them close, help them respect quieter moments and make sure they do not run across the dance floor.

Care for the room

Come feeling well, arrive freshly showered, keep your shirt on and wipe up any sweat you leave behind.

Take care

Listen to your body, respect the people around you and choose the level of movement that feels right. There are no steps to learn and no pressure to perform.
