OHMY GORD

A room to move in.

A conscious dance facilitator and DJ guide the session from arrival to landing.

Simone welcomes the room and offers a few clear invitations to help you arrive in your body. Then OHMY GORD builds a musical wave through warm grooves, surprising turns and full-bodied release before the room comes back down together. There is no choreography and no experience needed. Follow the guidance when it helps, leave it when it does not.
