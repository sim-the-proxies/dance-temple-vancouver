01 What is Dance Temple?

Come for
the music.
Move your way.

Dance Temple Vancouver is an all-ages dance floor for people who care about music and miss having somewhere to properly let go.

Each session is held by conscious dance facilitator Simone and one of our brilliant local DJs. Simone welcomes the room, offers a few simple prompts and helps everyone arrive and land together. The DJ shapes the musical arc, starting gently, building the energy, then bringing it back down. Together, they help you have fun, feel free and move your body consciously.

There is no choreography and no pressure to perform. Dance hard, move quietly, take a break, then jump back in. The guidance is there when it helps, and easy to leave when it does not.

Adults come for a real dance, not a children’s activity. Kids get to share the floor, not sit on the sidelines. Everyone looks after the space and gives each other room to move.

  • Free movementNo prescribed steps
  • Clear headsSubstance-free space
  • Real connectionPhones away on the floor
  • All agesKids and grown-ups share the floor
